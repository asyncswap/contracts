AsyncSwap

AsyncSwap is an on-chain order book and matching engine built as a Uniswap v4 hook. Traders set a limit price for assets paired with USDC. Orders match with other traders or with the pool's active liquidity, and anyone can permissionlessly fill a resting order.

  • Price, then bid, then time. Book matching ranks orders by price. Among orders at the same price, a higher escrowed USDC priority bid goes first, and earlier orders break equal bids.
  • Orders rest until they execute. Unfilled amounts stay open for later matching, a pool fill, a filler, repricing or cancellation.
  • No swap touches the curve directly. Every swap becomes an order. The only AMM swaps are the ones the hook makes to fill a resting order from pool liquidity, and those pay active LPs a 0.30% fee.
  • Proceeds you can always claim. A book match credits both sides as PoolManager claims, which you redeem to any address through TokenClaims (ERC-20) or NativeClaims (ETH). A recipient a token refuses cannot block matching.
