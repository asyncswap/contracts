Governance token (ASWAP)

ASWAP is the Async Swap governance token. It has one address on every chain it lives on, a fixed supply of 1,000,000,000, and voting, permits and flash loans built in. Manage it at governance.asyncswap.org: see supply on each chain, bridge ASWAP, track withdrawals and delegate votes.

The token was deployed with CREATE2, using the same bytecode, constructor argument and salt on each chain, so the address is identical everywhere. Use only this address: any other contract calling itself ASWAP is not the token.

Supply

The full 1B was minted once, on Base, at deployment. No one can mint more. Bridging burns ASWAP on the chain it leaves and mints the same amount on the chain it arrives at, so nothing is escrowed. The total across the four chains is always 1B. While a transfer is in flight, it has been burned on the source chain and not yet minted on the destination, so the per-chain totals can add up to less than 1B for that time.

Bridging

Each OP Stack standard bridge pairs ASWAP with itself (remoteToken() returns the token's own address), and Ethereum is the hub:

Loading diagram...
  • Into Ethereum takes about 7 days. Prove the withdrawal once the L2 has posted its output root (about an hour), then finalize it after the challenge window.
  • Out of Ethereum to Base, Optimism or Unichain takes minutes.
  • To move between two L2s, go through Ethereum.

The governance app runs all of these steps, including prove and finalize.

Who can mint and burn:

  • On Base, Optimism and Unichain: that chain's L2StandardBridge (0x4200000000000000000000000000000000000010) and the SuperchainTokenBridge (0x4200000000000000000000000000000000000028).
  • On Ethereum: the L1StandardBridge of Base, Optimism and Unichain. Each one mints only when it finalizes a withdrawal from its own chain, where the tokens were already burned.
  • On any other chain: no one.

Direct L2-to-L2 transfers through the SuperchainTokenBridge (ERC-7802) are built in and will work once Superchain interop is live on mainnet. Until then, use the routes above.

Voting

ASWAP implements ERC20Votes. Voting power comes from delegation, not from holding: a balance counts only after its holder delegates, either to someone else or to themselves. Delegate in the governance app, by calling delegate(delegatee), or without gas with a signed delegateBySig.

  • The votes clock is the block timestamp (CLOCK_MODE() returns mode=timestamp), so past voting power is read by time: getPastVotes(account, timepoint) and getPastTotalSupply(timepoint).
  • Votes are tracked separately on each chain, and count only the ASWAP held on that chain. Bridging moves the balance, and the voting power with it once the receiver has delegated on the destination chain.
  • No governor contract is deployed yet. The token records voting power for one to use.

Permits and flash loans

  • Permit (ERC-2612): approve with a signature instead of a transaction. The EIP-712 domain name is Async Swap.
  • Flash loans (ERC-3156): flashLoan mints ASWAP to the borrower and burns it when it is repaid in the same transaction. There is no fee.
