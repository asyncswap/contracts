Title: AsyncSwapFlashFiller

Base for flash fillers. Write _route; this contract handles the rest:

  • only the Router may call the callback, and only for a fill this contract started;
  • it approves the Router for the output owed to the maker;
  • it pays the exact MEV tax owed, from its ETH balance, so fill needs no msg.value. Keep enough ETH in the contract for the tax (send it with fill, or fund it beforehand). Surplus and refunds stay in the contract; withdraw sends them to the owner.

asyncSwapFlashFill

Called by the Router inside its PoolManager unlock, after the order was closed and its input, less the 0.02% trade fee, credited to the filler as ERC-6909 claims (amountIn of the input currency, id 0 for native ETH).

amountOwed is the order's minimum output plus any live Dutch auction premium; the Router pays the minimum to the hook and the premium to the maker. Before returning, the filler must hold amountOwed claims of the order's output currency and allow the Router to transfer them (PoolManager.approve(router, id, amountOwed) or an operator grant). Inside the callback it may swap on any v4 pool, burn/mint/take/settle, and fill other Async Swap orders through IAsyncSwapCallback.fillOrderInCallback and matchBookInCallback. Every delta it opens must be zero when the Router's unlock ends.

 
function asyncSwapFlashFill(AsyncOrder calldata order, uint256 amountIn, uint256 amountOwed, bytes calldata data)
