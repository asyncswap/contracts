# Async Swap

Async Swap is an MEV-capturing DEX: an on-chain order book and matching engine built as a Uniswap
v4 hook. Value that searchers usually take from ordering and backruns goes to the protocol and LPs
instead. Traders set a
limit price for assets paired with USDC. Orders match with other traders or with the pool's active
liquidity, and anyone can permissionlessly fill a resting order.

- **Price, then bid, then time.** Book matching ranks orders by price. Among orders at the same
  price, a higher escrowed USDC priority bid goes first, and earlier orders break equal bids.
- **Ordering MEV is auctioned, not extracted.** Priority bids are paid in USDC to the protocol's
  FeeVault, so jumping the queue at a price costs the bidder, not other traders.
- **Backruns pay LPs.** A pool fill that moves the price back within two blocks of another pool
  fill pays a dynamic fee of half the price gap it closes, split between LPs and the protocol. See
  [dynamic fee ordering](/protocol/dynamic-fee-ordering).
- **Orders rest until they execute.** Unfilled amounts stay open for later matching, a pool fill,
  a filler, repricing or cancellation.
- **No swap touches the curve directly.** Every swap becomes an order. The only AMM swaps are the
  ones the hook makes to fill a resting order from pool liquidity, and those pay active LPs
  0.02% and the protocol 0.01%. Book matches and filler fills take 0.02% of what each side receives.
- **Proceeds you can always claim.** A book match credits both sides as PoolManager claims, which
  you redeem to any address through `TokenClaims` (ERC-20) or `NativeClaims` (ETH), or to your own
  wallet through `Router.withdrawClaims`. A recipient a token refuses cannot block matching.
- **Rules the owner cannot change.** Anyone can open a USDC pool on the hook. The minimum order, the
  $0.10 priority-bid cap and the 0.0001-token asset floor are fixed when the hook is deployed; the
  owner can only collect fees, sweep stranded balances and transfer ownership.
- **Batch your actions.** `Router.multicall` cancels several orders and withdraws claims in one
  atomic transaction, with no extra approvals.
