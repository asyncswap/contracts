Title: MatchingEngine

Standalone price-time matching harness. It maintains a radix-indexed limit book and emits deterministic fill instructions.

Algorithm and gas benchmark only: it does not custody or settle tokens. Live orders settle through the AsyncSwap hook and AsyncMatching.
